# Resume framing

## One paragraph

Ablated a retrieval stack across three BEIR datasets (scifact, nfcorpus, fiqa;
1,271 test queries over 66k documents), measuring what BM25, dense bi-encoders,
hybrid fusion and cross encoder reranking each contribute in nDCG@10 against
measured p95 latency and cost per 1000 queries. A BM25 plus bge-base score
blend, with its weight tuned on dev and reported on test, beat BM25 by 0.109,
0.078 and 0.204 nDCG@10 on the three datasets, all outside the paired bootstrap
interval. The result worth reporting was negative: adding a cross encoder over
the top 100 made the ranking measurably *worse* on all three (0.067, 0.024 and
0.045 nDCG@10 lost, intervals excluding zero) while multiplying p95 latency, and
Recall@100 explained why on nfcorpus. Shipped a hand built taxonomy of 84
labelled failures showing that the majority of scifact and nfcorpus "failures"
are label provenance rather than retrieval error, and an audit of my own prose
that caught four wrong comparative claims before publication.

## What to say if asked what it shows

The layer everyone adds last was the layer that should not have been added. A
`ms-marco-MiniLM` cross encoder improves a BM25 first stage but degrades a
`bge-base` hybrid one, because a reranker's value is not a property of the
reranker, it is the gap between the reranker and the first stage under it. The
degradation is monotone in k: reranking 100 candidates is worse than reranking
10, on all three datasets.

## What to say if asked what went wrong

Three latency measurement bugs, all found by noticing numbers that did not make
sense rather than by a test failing. Insufficient GPU warmup made the first
dataset timed look 5x slower than the last. Timing one model at a time gave
whichever ran first the cold start, which reported the 22M parameter encoder as
slower than the 110M one. A fourth measurement behaved in a way I could not
explain, so I deleted it rather than ship a confident story around it.

Then a prose audit against the data found four wrong hand written claims,
including one that inverted an engineering conclusion: I had explained
nfcorpus's low Recall@100 as an artifact of the 100 document cutoff, when the
cutoff caps it at 0.96 and the real cause is the retriever missing judged
documents. Every error was in a sentence comparing two things. None were in the
generated numbers, which is the argument for generating them.
